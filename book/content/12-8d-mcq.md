### Forty further aptitude questions, with worked answers

Each question is single-answer with four options. Attempt a question in 60 seconds, mark the option, and only then read the working; the topics follow the order of [§12.2](#s12-2){.ref} to [§12.6](#s12-6){.ref}.

::: problems
::: q
[Number system]{.meta}

**M1.** Remainder when 7<sup>100</sup> is divided by 6? (a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 5

**Answer (a).** 7 ≡ 1 (mod 6), so 7<sup>100</sup> ≡ 1.
:::

::: q
[Number system]{.meta}

**M2.** Largest three-digit number divisible by 13? (a) 988 (b) 989 (c) 990 (d) 999

**Answer (a).** 999 = 13 × 76 + 11, so 999 − 11 = 988.
:::

::: q
[Number system]{.meta}

**M3.** Sum of the first 20 odd numbers? (a) 380 (b) 400 (c) 420 (d) 441

**Answer (b).** The sum of the first <i>n</i> odd numbers is <i>n</i><sup>2</sup> = 400.
:::

::: q
[Number system]{.meta}

**M4.** How many multiples of 7 lie between 100 and 300? (a) 27 (b) 28 (c) 29 (d) 30

**Answer (b).** First 105 = 7 × 15, last 294 = 7 × 42, so 42 − 15 + 1 = 28.
:::

::: q
[HCF and LCM]{.meta}

**M5.** The HCF of two numbers is 8 and their LCM is 240. One number is 48. The other is? (a) 40 (b) 60 (c) 80 (d) 120

**Answer (a).** 8 × 240 / 48 = 40.
:::

::: q
[HCF and LCM]{.meta}

**M6.** The smallest number divisible by 15, 20 and 25? (a) 150 (b) 300 (c) 400 (d) 600

**Answer (b).** 15 = 3·5, 20 = 2<sup>2</sup>·5, 25 = 5<sup>2</sup>; LCM = 2<sup>2</sup> × 3 × 5<sup>2</sup> = 300.
:::

::: q
[Percentages]{.meta}

**M7.** If 40% of <i>x</i> equals 60% of <i>y</i>, then <i>x</i> : <i>y</i> is? (a) 2 : 3 (b) 3 : 2 (c) 4 : 6 (d) 3 : 4

**Answer (b).** 0.4<i>x</i> = 0.6<i>y</i> gives <i>x</i>/<i>y</i> = 6/4 = 3 : 2.
:::

::: q
[Percentages]{.meta}

**M8.** A's salary is 25% more than B's. By what percentage is B's less than A's? (a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 16.67% (d) 24%

**Answer (a).** Let B = 100, A = 125; difference 25 on base 125 gives 20%.
:::

::: q
[Percentages]{.meta}

**M9.** A town of 20,000 grows 10% a year. Population after two years? (a) 24,000 (b) 24,200 (c) 24,400 (d) 22,000

**Answer (b).** 20,000 × 1.1 × 1.1 = 24,200.
:::

::: q
[Profit and loss]{.meta}

**M10.** An article costs ₹250. At what marked price does a 20% discount still leave a 20% profit? (a) ₹360 (b) ₹375 (c) ₹400 (d) ₹300

**Answer (b).** Required selling price 250 × 1.2 = 300; marked price × 0.8 = 300 gives 375.
:::

::: q
[Profit and loss]{.meta}

**M11.** Selling at ₹440 gives a loss of 12%. The cost price? (a) ₹480 (b) ₹500 (c) ₹520 (d) ₹388

**Answer (b).** 440 / 0.88 = 500.
:::

::: q
[Profit and loss]{.meta}

**M12.** Two articles are each sold at ₹1,200, one at 20% gain and one at 20% loss. Net result? (a) No loss (b) 4% loss (c) 4% gain (d) 2% loss

**Answer (b).** Costs 1,000 and 1,500, total 2,500; sales 2,400; loss 100 = 4%.
:::

::: q
[Ratio]{.meta}

**M13.** ₹1,210 is divided in the ratio 2 : 3 : 6. The largest share? (a) ₹220 (b) ₹330 (c) ₹660 (d) ₹550

**Answer (c).** Parts total 11, one part is ₹110, largest is 6 × 110.
:::

::: q
[Ratio]{.meta}

**M14.** A 60-litre mixture has milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1. Water to add to make it 1 : 1? (a) 10 L (b) 15 L (c) 20 L (d) 30 L

**Answer (c).** Milk 40 L, water 20 L; add 20 L to equalise.
:::

[Averages]{.meta}

**M15.** The average of 7 numbers is 15. An eighth number raises the average to 16. The eighth number? (a) 21 (b) 22 (c) 23 (d) 24

**Answer (c).** 8 × 16 − 7 × 15 = 128 − 105 = 23.
:::

[Averages]{.meta}

**M16.** Average of the first 10 even numbers? (a) 10 (b) 11 (c) 12 (d) 10.5

**Answer (b).** Sum = 10 × 11 = 110, so the average is 11.

[Time and work]{.meta}

**M17.** 6 men finish a job in 8 days. How long for 12 men? (a) 2 days (b) 4 days (c) 6 days (d) 16 days

**Answer (b).** Man-days = 48; 48/12 = 4.
:::

::: q
[Time and work]{.meta}

**M18.** A takes 30 days and B takes 45 days. Together? (a) 15 days (b) 18 days (c) 20 days (d) 75 days

**Answer (b).** 30 × 45 / 75 = 18.
:::

::: q
[Time and work]{.meta}

**M19.** A is twice as efficient as B. Together they take 12 days. B alone takes? (a) 18 days (b) 24 days (c) 36 days (d) 48 days

**Answer (c).** Rates 2<i>k</i> and <i>k</i>: 3<i>k</i> = 1/12, so <i>k</i> = 1/36.
:::

::: q
[Pipes]{.meta}

**M20.** A pipe fills a tank in 12 hours and a drain empties it in 18 hours. Both open: time to fill? (a) 30 h (b) 36 h (c) 6 h (d) 72 h

**Answer (b).** 1/12 − 1/18 = 1/36.
:::

[Speed and distance]{.meta}

**M21.** 90 km/h in m/s? (a) 15 (b) 20 (c) 25 (d) 30

**Answer (c).** 90 × 5/18 = 25.
:::

::: q
[Speed and distance]{.meta}

**M22.** Walking at 5 km/h a man is 12 minutes late; at 6 km/h he is 10 minutes early. Distance? (a) 9 km (b) 10 km (c) 11 km (d) 12 km

**Answer (c).** <i>d</i>/5 − <i>d</i>/6 = 22/60 h gives <i>d</i>/30 = 11/30, <i>d</i> = 11.
:::

::: q
[Speed and distance]{.meta}

**M23.** Two trains 100 km apart start towards each other at 40 and 60 km/h. They meet after? (a) 30 min (b) 45 min (c) 1 h (d) 1.5 h

**Answer (c).** Closing speed 100 km/h, gap 100 km.
:::

::: q
[Trains]{.meta}

**M24.** A 180 m train crosses a pole in 9 s. Its speed? (a) 54 km/h (b) 60 km/h (c) 72 km/h (d) 80 km/h

**Answer (c).** 180/9 = 20 m/s = 72 km/h.
:::

::: q
[Trains]{.meta}

**M25.** Trains of 120 m and 130 m run the same way at 60 and 40 km/h. Time to overtake? (a) 30 s (b) 45 s (c) 50 s (d) 60 s

**Answer (b).** Relative 20 km/h = 50/9 m/s; 250 / (50/9) = 45.
:::

[Boats]{.meta}

**M26.** A boat does 12 km/h in still water; the stream is 4 km/h. Time to go 16 km upstream? (a) 1 h (b) 1.5 h (c) 2 h (d) 4 h

**Answer (c).** Upstream 8 km/h; 16/8 = 2.
:::

[Boats]{.meta}

**M27.** A boat does 15 km/h downstream and 9 km/h upstream. Still-water speed? (a) 10 km/h (b) 11 km/h (c) 12 km/h (d) 13 km/h

**Answer (c).** (15 + 9)/2 = 12.
:::

**Answer (b).** 5,000 × 6 × 3 / 100 = 900.

**M30.** At simple interest a sum doubles in 10 years. The annual rate? (a) 5% (b) 8% (c) 10% (d) 12.5%
